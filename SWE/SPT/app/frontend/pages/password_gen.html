What makes a password strong?

  • Longer is better: Aim for at least 12-16 characters.
  • Use a variety of characters: Combine uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols when appropriate.
  • Use randomness: Randomly generated passwords are harder to guess than predictable patterns.
