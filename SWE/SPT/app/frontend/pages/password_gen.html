What Makes a Password Strong?

  • Longer is better: Aim for at least 12-16 characters.
  • Use a variety of characters: Combine uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols when appropriate.
  • Use randomness: Randomly generated passwords are harder to guess than predictable patterns.

Why Use a Password Generator?

  • Creates random, unpredictable passwords without relying on patterns you might normally choose.
  • Makes it easy to generate long and complex passwords.
  • Reduces the temptation to reuse simple passwords across multiple accounts. Reusing passwords give threat actors more accounts to to attack.
  • Saves time compared with manually creating strong passwords.

How to Keep Generated Passwords Secure

  • Use a reputable password manager to securely store and manage your passwords.
  • Protect your password manager with a strong, unique master password.
  • Never share your passwords with other people.
  • Don't store passwords in plain-text files, notes, or spreadsheets.
  • Enable MFA/2FA on accounts whenever available.
  • If a password is exposed or compromised, change it immediately and don't reuse it elsewhere.
